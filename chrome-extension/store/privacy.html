GetAIPhotoEditor Chrome Extension — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

The GetAIPhotoEditor Chrome extension does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

  • Images you select are previewed locally in the popup only. They are not uploaded by the extension and are discarded when the popup closes.
  • Prompt text stays in the popup and is not sent anywhere by the extension.
  • The Open button opens https://getaiphotoeditor.com in a new tab. Use of the website is covered by the site privacy policy.
  • The extension requests no special Chrome permissions.

Site privacy policy: https://getaiphotoeditor.com/privacy-policy
