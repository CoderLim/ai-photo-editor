## Name

## Short description (≤132 characters)

Upload photos, draft a prompt, then open GetAIPhotoEditor to remove backgrounds, retouch, and enhance.

## Detailed description

GetAIPhotoEditor is a companion for getaiphotoeditor.com.

Use this extension to:

- Select photos (local preview only)
- Draft a prompt for your next edit
- Open GetAIPhotoEditor in one click to remove backgrounds, retouch portraits, erase objects, and enhance images on the website

This version does not generate or edit images inside Chrome. Clicking Open opens https://getaiphotoeditor.com. Your selected files stay on your device and are not uploaded by the extension.

Website: https://getaiphotoeditor.com
Support: support@getaiphotoeditor.com
