# SEO Content — $ARGUMENTS

1. Check actual functionality in the codebase and UI first. Verify credit costs, upload formats, outputs, account requirements, data handling and model behavior. Do not claim unsupported features.
2. Research the current SERP and users' primary intent. Identify related terms and a distinct topic cluster; avoid copying competitors or fabricating volumes and keyword difficulty.
3. Audit the site's existing pages for overlapping intent and potential cannibalization. Map one main intent to each dedicated route.
4. Write an accurate title, meta description, H1, helpful H2s, steps, real use cases and FAQ. Keep copy specific and readable; avoid keyword density formulas and keyword stuffing.
5. Use existing design patterns, a working editor or tool, canonical metadata, truthful structured data, relevant contextual internal links and sitemap/nav entries.
6. Add concrete usage limitations (e.g. transparency and fine edges). Prefer verifiable, qualified claims over guarantees of perfect results.
7. Verify the published content against the working feature. Run the build and typecheck when possible; otherwise use PR CI and disclose limitations.

Background photo pages must distinguish (a) foreground cutout to transparent PNG, (b) composing a cutout over a selected image/color, and (c) AI scene generation from a text prompt.
