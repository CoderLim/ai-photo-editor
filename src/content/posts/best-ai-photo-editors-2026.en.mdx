## How to choose an AI photo editor

Match the tool to the edit, not the brand name:

- **Prompt / chat edits** — You describe the change in words ([remove an object](/tools/remove-object), [swap a background](/tools/change-background), soften light).
- **Brush / selection edits** — You mark the area, then generate or fill.
- **Design-first** — The photo sits inside a poster, ad, or social layout.
- **Single-job specialists** — Cutouts for listings, or noise/upscale recovery.
- **Desktop precision** — Layers, masks, and print-critical control.

If you only need one cleanup a week, a [browser AI photo editor](/#editor) is usually enough. If you retouch for clients every day, a desktop suite still earns its keep.

## 2. Adobe Photoshop (with Firefly) — Best for precision control

![Adobe Photoshop homepage screenshot](/images/ai-photo-editor/blog/best-ai-photo-editors/photoshop-firefly.webp)

Adobe still owns the “I need exact control” lane. Firefly-powered tools such as generative fill, expand, and object removal sit inside a familiar layer-and-mask environment. Web Firefly is also useful when you want a cleanup without opening the full desktop app.

**Pros**

- Masks, layers, and undo depth for client-critical work
- Generative tools sit next to classic retouching
- Strong ecosystem if you already shoot or design in Adobe apps

**Cons**

- Subscription cost adds up for light users
- More UI than you need for a one-off background swap

**Best for:** retouchers and designers who must correct AI output by hand.

**Skip it if:** you only edit a few photos a month and a [browser AI photo editor](/#editor) like [GetAIPhotoEditor](/#editor) covers those jobs.

## 3. Canva — Best for editing photos inside designs

![Canva AI photo editing homepage screenshot](/images/ai-photo-editor/blog/best-ai-photo-editors/canva.webp)

Canva is a design tool first. Its AI photo features (erase, expand, generate, resize) matter most when the photo is one piece of a larger layout—ads, carousels, pitch decks, thumbnails.

**Pros**

- Templates and brand kits next to the photo
- Easy for teams that already live in Canva
- Quick turnaround for social and marketing assets

**Cons**

- Free AI quotas and some removal tools sit behind paid plans
- Not the deepest choice for portrait or product retouch alone

**Best for:** marketers and social teams who edit photos as part of a finished design.

**Skip it if:** free [background removal](/ai-background-remover) is the only reason you are shopping—try a dedicated [AI background remover](/ai-background-remover) or [background changer](/photo-background-changer) instead.

## 4. Photoroom — Best for product photos and cutouts

![Photoroom homepage screenshot](/images/ai-photo-editor/blog/best-ai-photo-editors/photoroom.webp)

Photoroom focuses on marketplace-ready product images: fast cutouts, clean backgrounds, and listing-friendly exports. Free plans often meter exports; AI background styles may use a separate credit meter.

**Pros**

- Strong subject cutouts for catalogs and marketplaces
- Mobile + web for sellers who shoot on phones
- Templates aimed at product presentation

**Cons**

- Export and AI meters need watching on free tiers
- Less ideal as a general scenery or portrait prompt editor

**Best for:** ecommerce sellers and anyone building a consistent product catalog look.

**Skip it if:** you mostly edit people, travel scenes, or creative composites—an [AI photo editor](/#editor) with prompts is a better fit.

## 6. Topaz Photo AI — Best for noise reduction and upscaling

![Topaz Labs Photo AI homepage screenshot](/images/ai-photo-editor/blog/best-ai-photo-editors/topaz.webp)

## FAQ

### What is the best AI photo editor for beginners?

A browser tool with short prompts is usually the easiest start. [GetAIPhotoEditor](/#editor) is aimed at that path: upload, describe, generate, review. New visitors can try the [AI photo editor](/#editor) with starter credits. If your work is mostly product cutouts, Photoroom may feel more direct.

### Are free AI photo editors good enough?

Yes for trials and light use—if you accept watermarks, export caps, or daily limits. [GetAIPhotoEditor](/#editor) lets you start with credits and buy more on the [pricing page](/pricing) when you need them. For client deliveries, check output size, watermarks, and whether your plan allows commercial use.

### Can AI photo editors replace Photoshop?

For many everyday cleanups, yes—an online [AI photo editor](/#editor) is enough for object removal, backgrounds, and light retouch. For layered composites, print color, and pixel-perfect masks, desktop precision tools still win. AI is a speed layer, not always the final authority.

### How do I get better AI edit results?

Be specific. Name the subject to keep, the change you want, and what must not move. After generate, zoom faces, edges, logos, and text.
