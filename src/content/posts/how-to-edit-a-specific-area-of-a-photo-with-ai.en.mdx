You edit a specific area of a photo with AI by **naming that area in plain language**, saying what should change there, and locking everything else. On [GetAIPhotoEditor](/#editor) you upload the photo, write that instruction, pick a quality, generate, then inspect pixels outside the target before you download.

## Area edit is not always a brush

Many tools answer this question with a mask: paint the region, then inpaint. That works when the product ships a brush. GetAIPhotoEditor’s live editor does **not** offer a precision-mask UI today. The shipped path is **prompt localization**: you tell the model which object or region to touch and what must stay unchanged.

The underlying model is GPT Image 2.6 Flare (the screen says “GPT Image 2.6”). There is no Flare versus Sunburst switch and no complexity control in the product. Quality is the fidelity dial you choose. For API-level Flare, Sunburst, and complexity, see [What Is ChatGPT Images 2.6?](/blog/what-is-chatgpt-images-2-6).

## Prompt pattern for local edits

Use three clauses in one instruction:

1. **Change only X** — name the object or region.
2. **Preserve X’s material** — texture, folds, fit, lighting, or material.
3. **Keep Y unchanged** — face, logo, product shape, pose, and the rest of the frame.

Examples you can adapt:

- Remove only the passerby behind the subject. Reconstruct the background naturally. Keep the subject’s face, clothing, and pose unchanged.
- Replace only the wall behind the product with a soft studio gray. Keep the product shape, label, and reflections unchanged.
- Change only the handbag from black leather to cognac brown. Preserve the stitching, hardware, and lighting. Keep the model and background unchanged.

For a broader upload–prompt–download loop, see [How to Edit Photos with AI](/blog/how-to-edit-photos-with-ai). For lettering already baked into a photo, use the [photo text editor](/photo-text-editor) and quote the new words exactly.

## What to check after you generate

- Face identity and expression
- Hair edges against the background
- Earrings, logos, and small accessories
- Background bokeh and table objects
- Material continuity on the edited region (knit, leather, paint)

If something that should have stayed still has moved, rewrite the “keep unchanged” list and generate again. Raising quality can improve detail; it does not guarantee a stricter region lock.

## FAQ

### Do I need a mask to edit one area?

Not on GetAIPhotoEditor today. Name the area in the prompt and state what must stay. Mask/inpaint UIs on other products are a different workflow.

### Which model does GetAIPhotoEditor use for this?

GPT Image 2.6 Flare on the edit path. The UI label is “GPT Image 2.6.” It does not expose Sunburst or complexity.

### Does higher quality lock the region better?

Not reliably. Higher quality spends more credits for finer detail. Region control still comes from a clear “change only / keep unchanged” prompt and from reviewing the full result.

### Can I edit text in one part of the photo?

Yes—describe the lettering change and what layout must stay, or use the dedicated [photo text editor](/photo-text-editor) and put the new words in quotation marks.

### Will the rest of the photo stay pixel-identical?

No. Expect small unintended shifts. Compare before and after outside the target every time.
